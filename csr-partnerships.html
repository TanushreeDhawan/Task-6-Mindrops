SADBHAAV CHILD DEVELOPMENT FOUNDATION

Partnering to Build a More Inclusive Future

Every child deserves the opportunity to communicate, learn, grow, and live independently. At Sadbhaav Child Development Foundation, we work every day to provide evidence-based therapies and holistic support to children with autism, developmental delays, ADHD, learning disabilities, cerebral palsy, Down syndrome, and other neurodevelopmental conditions.

Creating lasting change requires meaningful partnerships. We invite corporates, foundations, and institutions to join us through Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives and become partners in transforming children's lives.

Partner With Us

Why Partner with Sadbhaav?

Today, Sadbhaav serves 180+ children through a multidisciplinary team of therapists, psychologists, educators, and specialists. Every year, we deliver thousands of therapy sessions, parent training programs, developmental assessments, and educational interventions to help children reach their fullest potential.
