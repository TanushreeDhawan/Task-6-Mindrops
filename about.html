SADBHAAV CHILD DEVELOPMENT FOUNDATION

Sadbhaav Child
Development Foundation

CREATING BRIGHTER FUTURES THROUGH EARLY SUPPORT AND INCLUSIVE CARE

Sadbhaav Child Development Foundation is a child development center dedicated to supporting children with developmental needs and guiding their families. We offer early intervention, therapy, and inclusive education through a compassionate, multidisciplinary approach.

Our team of therapists, educators, and psychologists works collaboratively to foster each child's growth. Sadbhaav Child Development Foundation is a trusted space for care, progress, and meaningful support.

Our Journey

From a single room and three members to a multidisciplinary team of over thirty — here is how Sadbhaav grew.

Our Mission

Delivering one-on-one therapy services tailored to individual developmental needs.

Offering pre-vocational and vocational training in small, focused groups.

Organizing training workshops for therapists, educators, and professionals.

Providing internships and observer programs for students and early-career professionals.

Conducting structured parent training programs for better home support.

Running community awareness initiatives on parenting, mental health, and developmental disorders.

Our Vision

We aim to offer early identification and intervention to children at-risk of Atypical development.

We aim to bridge the gaps in their developmental potential and functional skills.

We aim to create support systems and networks to assist their daily challenges.

We aim to create awareness and acceptance in society for all atypical development and challenges thereof.

What We Do

At Sadbhaav Child Development Foundation, we support children with developmental needs to grow

Therapy

We provide one-on-one speech, occupational, and behavioural therapies to help children strengthen communication, movement, and everyday skills.

Early Intervention

We offer structured support in the early years when development is most crucial, helping children build strong foundations for the future.

Inclusive Learning

Every child learns in their own way. Our programs adapt to their pace and strengths, building confidence and encouraging meaningful progress.

Functional movements

Through functional movements, children build motor skills, improve posture, enhance focus, and gain confidence in daily tasks and play.
